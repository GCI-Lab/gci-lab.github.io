About the Lab

Addressing the critical sustainability paradox of accelerating digital transformation against its environmental footprint.

Our Vision

Introducing Green Computing research initiatives nationally and internationally to pave the way toward carbon-neutral digital systems.

Our Mission

Conducting cutting-edge research in the field of Green Computing, spanning green hardware and software-based system design using Smart IoT, Edge Computing, Cloud Computing, and Cyber-Physical Systems.

Core Objectives

Research Excellence

Pursue applied research in energy-efficient computing and publish in high-impact, peer-reviewed venues.

Student Training

Supervise MSc projects and conduct advanced seminars to nurture the next generation of sustainable IT leaders.

Interdisciplinary

Collaborating across engineering, environmental science, and healthcare on integrated smart systems.

Industry Engagement

Partnering with telecom, energy, and government sectors to translate research into real-world green ICT solutions.
